QHE-BHE Correspondence

Numerical investigation of the Quantum Hall Effect — Berry Holonomy Effect correspondence on triangulated spheres.

Project Overview

This project explores the correspondence between the Quantum Hall Effect (QHE) and the Berry Holonomy Effect (BHE) on triangulated spheres. Through six phases of analysis — from Platonic solids to Monte Carlo scaling — we investigate the geometric and topological properties of quantum states on discrete manifolds.

Key Areas

  • Platonic solid geometries and their quantum properties
  • Triangulated sphere discretization
  • Berry phase and holonomy calculations
  • Monte Carlo scaling analysis
  • Finite-size scaling and critical phenomena
