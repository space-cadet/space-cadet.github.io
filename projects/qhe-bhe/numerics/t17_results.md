## Key Findings

1. **Some resonance graphs are disconnected** — Topological degeneracy may exist.
2. **Spectral gap exists** — The gap ranges from 0.1132 to 2.0000.
3. **Average degree increases with N_p** — Larger graphs have more connected neighbors.

## Connection to QHE

The spectral gap of the resonance graph is the analog of the QHE energy gap. A connected, gapped resonance graph corresponds to a unique RVB ground state with topological order (QHE-like behavior).
